MAZE & PATHFINDER

Generate a maze, then watch four classic search algorithms work their way through it one step at a time. Draw your own walls, add terrain costs, and see exactly where each one wastes its effort.

Press Solve to start a search.

Click or drag on the grid to add and remove walls by hand. Green shading is the order cells were expanded — darker means earlier. With flat terrain, Dijkstra and breadth-first search expand cells in the same order, which is the point: Dijkstra only earns its keep once moves cost different amounts. Greedy best-first is fast but its route is not guaranteed to be the cheapest; A* is, as long as the heuristic never overestimates.
